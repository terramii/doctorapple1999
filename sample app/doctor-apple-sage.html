MOH Directive 2026: Mandatory Identity Checks at Reception Counters
Category: Regulatory | MOH Singapore
All registered medical practitioners and counter personnel are reminded that physical identification checks must be conducted in person at Counter Reception before admitting patients. Pre-registration systems (e.g. SurveyJS / pre-fills) are valid for demographic compilation, but require confirmation check marks on the reception console.
PDPA Regulations for Healthcare Providers: Securing Electronic Health Records (EHR)
Category: Privacy Compliance | PDPC Advisory
Any patient clinical survey data, address particulars, and NRIC keys stored in digital caches must be encrypted at rest. Double check that NNL/NLU parsers are clean of secondary data copies.
